Revolutionising Beauty & Wellness Education


The beauty industry is growing fast! New trends appear every day, techniques improve, and the demand for skilled professionals increases simultaneously. Are you ready to join this exciting world?

Orane, India’s leading beauty academy chain, is your gateway to a thriving career. With over 1 Lakh+ students already trained and counting, we’ve empowered countless individuals to turn their passion for beauty and wellness into a fulfilling profession. At present, 110+ Orane beauty and wellness academies are present throughout India, and their number is increasing at a steady pace. Along with these beauty schools, Orane has a presence in Canada, with one world-class beauty college.

We understand the importance of staying ahead of the curve when it comes to training and preparing our enrolled students for a successful future. That is why we provide a wide range of beauty courses in various domains, including makeup, hairstyling, skincare, and nail art, as well as professional nutrition courses offering beginner to advanced certifications.

Furthermore, with the aim of imparting specific skills, Orane also offers online courses at affordable prices, allowing students to gain knowledge while sitting in the comfort of their own homes.
